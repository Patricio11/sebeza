## 3 · What YouTube is FOR in our system

Two things, in order:

1. **Shorts now**: the same vertical videos as TikTok/Reels, zero extra production. Shorts
   feed YouTube's recommendation engine and quietly build subscribers while we build the
   product.
2. **Long-form later** (post-launch): this is the real reason to hold the channel. When the
   doors open, YouTube is where "How to build your Sebenza profile" (in four languages),
   employer walkthroughs, and TVET/SETA explainer content live: searchable, permanent,
   linkable from the product itself. Searching for help on YouTube is deeply embedded
   behaviour in SA; we will meet it.

No long-form before there's something a user can act on; an empty tutorial channel helps
nobody.

## 5 · Rules of the channel

- Same production rules as the video bank: captioned, no faces for now, no meme audio, no PII,
  clean exports (never the TikTok watermark; YouTube down-ranks it).
- Comments: hold to the same reply discipline as everywhere; YouTube comments compound like
  the videos do.
- Playlists from day one: `The build` (V4, V11 episodes) and `What is Sebenza` (V1, V2, V5,
  V8, V9): playlists are how YouTube surfaces small channels.
