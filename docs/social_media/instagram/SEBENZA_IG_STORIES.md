# SEBENZA · INSTAGRAM STORIES
**2-3 stories a week. Informal, sticker-driven, gone in 24 hours. This is where followers become community.**

Stories don't need to be polished posts; they need to be *present*. The pre-rendered cards keep
even the quick ones on-brand, and Instagram's native stickers (poll, question, quiz) do the
engagement work.

## THE CARDS (1080x1920, safe-zoned, stripe at the bottom)

| File | What it is | How to use |
|---|---|---|
| `assets/story-intro.png` | The lockup + "to work" | Day one, and again whenever new followers arrive in a wave. |
| `assets/story-question.png` | "If you could change one thing about job-hunting in SA?" with an empty panel | Post it, then **place Instagram's QUESTION sticker directly over the dashed panel**. Reshare the best answers (they arrive anonymous-to-viewers; never reshare anything identifying). |
| `assets/story-free-always.png` | "Free for job-seekers. Always." | Pair with a POLL sticker: "Should job platforms ever charge seekers?" Yes/Never. |
| `assets/story-trust.png` | "Trust is the whole product." | Pair with a QUIZ sticker: "What does an unverified badge mean on Sebenza?" (Answer: exactly that. We never fake it.) |
| `assets/story-scam-warning.png` | The scam PSA | Run it, then add the "Add yours" / share nudge in the caption line. This one is genuinely worth resharing monthly. |

**Sticker placement rule:** all five cards keep the top and bottom ~250px clear (Instagram's
camera/reply UI lives there). Put stickers in the middle band, ideally over or under the
headline, never over the footer lockup.

## AD-HOC STORIES (the in-between ones, phone-made)

- A clean screenshot of something just built + one line of text in Hanken-style plain text.
- "This week we're working on ______" + a POLL or EMOJI-SLIDER sticker.
- Reshare the page's own feed post to the story the day it publishes (one tap; drives the feed).
- A COUNTDOWN sticker when there's something honest to count down to (a feature going live).

Rules: our colours or clean screenshots; no faces for now; no blurry text-on-default-white; the
same honesty guardrails as everywhere else.

## HIGHLIGHTS (permanent story folders on the profile)

After the first few weeks, create three highlights so the best stories outlive 24 hours:
1. **About** (cover: the S avatar): intro story + the promise cards.
2. **You said** (question-sticker answers, anonymised): the community's voice.
3. **Building** (build moments, screenshots): the proof-it's-real archive.

Highlight covers: reuse the story cards themselves; Instagram lets you pick the cover frame.
