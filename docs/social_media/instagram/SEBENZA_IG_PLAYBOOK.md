# SEBENZA · INSTAGRAM PLAYBOOK
**How Instagram differs from Facebook, and how we use it without doubling the workload.**
*Companions: `SEBENZA_IG_START_HERE.md`, `SEBENZA_IG_POSTS.md`, `SEBENZA_IG_STORIES.md`. The
Facebook playbook's gate, guardrails, and voice rules all apply verbatim.*

---

## WHY INSTAGRAM AT ALL

- **The audience skews younger.** Youth unemployment is the heart of the mission; this is where
  that audience actually is.
- **Our identity is Instagram-native.** The cards are already 4:5, the grid will look like a
  designed publication from day one. Very few SA employment brands look like anything on IG;
  the bar is low and our ceiling is high.
- **Two formats Facebook doesn't reward as much**: Stories (direct, informal, sticker-driven
  engagement with people who already follow) and Reels (the only real organic-reach engine
  Meta still runs).

## THE DIVISION OF LABOUR (so it stays one person's workload)

| Surface | What it's for | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Feed** (3/week) | The brand shelf. Same three weekly posts as Facebook, same graphics, captions trimmed for IG. | Near zero: cross-post days, adapt the caption. |
| **Stories** (2-3/week) | Warmth + engagement with existing followers. Polls, questions, behind-the-scenes moments. Gone in 24h, so they can be informal. | Minutes each; cards pre-rendered. |
| **Carousels** (~1/month) | Depth. The "swipe" format for anything with steps or lists: the promises, how it works, scam red flags. | One render run per carousel. |
| **Reels** (1/week when possible) | Reach. 15-30s screen recordings of the product doing something real, phone-recorded build moments, captioned. | The only new content type IG asks of us. |

## CAPTION CRAFT (IG is not FB)

- **The first line is everything.** IG truncates at ~125 characters with "... more". Lead with
  the hook, never with "Sebenza. In isiZulu..." context-setting (that's the FB opener; on IG,
  flip it).
- **Shorter overall.** FB captions are stories; IG captions are the story's best two paragraphs.
- **Links don't work in captions.** Never paste a URL; say "link in bio" when it matters.
- **Hashtags: 3-5, at the end of the caption.** IG's own guidance since 2024: hashtags are
  topical labels, not a reach hack. Same core set: `#Sebenza #SebenzaSA #Mzansi` + 1-2 topical.
- **Emoji:** same restraint as FB. One or two, where they land.

## CROSS-POSTING MECHANICS

Schedule both platforms from **Meta Business Suite** (one composer, both accounts, scheduled
slots). Two rules:
1. Never auto-crosspost a Facebook post containing a link or "see the About section"; IG
   captions can't carry either.
2. Rewrite the first line for IG (hook first), keep the body.

## REELS: THE FORMAT, HONESTLY

We hold the same bar as everything else: real product, real build, no trends-chasing, no
dancing, no AI voiceovers. What a Sebenza Reel is:
- A 15-30s screen recording: a search happening, the Career Compass showing a gap, a profile
  going live. Captioned in-app (many watch muted), our colours, one idea.
- A build moment: the terminal running tests, a migration applying, a feature working the
  first time. Dev content performs shockingly well with young SA tech audiences.
- Cut in the IG app or CapCut; no production values needed. The honesty IS the aesthetic.

## ENGAGEMENT (10 minutes a day, same as FB)

Reply to every comment and every story reply. Answer DMs (Business Suite unifies FB + IG
inboxes). Follow and genuinely engage with SA youth/careers/TVET/tech accounts. Never buy
followers, never follow-for-follow; a small real audience beats a big fake one, and the
account's honesty is the brand's honesty.

## GUARDRAILS

Everything from the Facebook playbook, plus:
- ❌ No link-in-caption workarounds (link shorteners in comments, etc.); "link in bio" only.
- ❌ No trend-audio Reels that would embarrass the institutional brand later.
- ❌ Stories are informal, never sloppy: the pre-rendered cards or clean phone shots, not
  blurry screenshots with default white text.
- ✅ Do use IG's native stickers (poll, question, quiz, countdown); that's what stories are for.

*Same story, second stage. The feed does the talking; stories do the listening; Reels do the
