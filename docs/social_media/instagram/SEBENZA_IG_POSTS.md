# SEBENZA · INSTAGRAM FEED BANK
**Feed posts, the promise carousel, and Reel ideas. Stories live in `SEBENZA_IG_STORIES.md`.**

The feed reuses the Facebook bank: **same graphics** (already 4:5), same three-per-week rhythm,
captions trimmed for Instagram. Rule of thumb: take the FB caption, move its best line to the
top (IG truncates at ~125 chars, the first line must hook), cut a third, drop any link or
"About section" mention, hashtags at the end.

## HOW TO PUBLISH (same two halves as Facebook)

Caption in the text box, graphic attached, 3-5 hashtags on the last line. Never a URL in the
caption; "link in bio" instead. For carousels: add all slides in order, cover first.

## REEL IDEAS (one a week, when there's something real to show)

1. **The search, live.** Screen recording: type "electrician · Gauteng", results appear,
   freshness chips visible. Text overlay: "Talent, findable." 15s.
2. **The Career Compass gap.** A profile's skills vs what employers nearby want; the
   "what to learn next" recommendation appears. Overlay: "It tells you what to learn next." 20s.
3. **Built for a R1500 phone.** Side-by-side: the app on a budget Android on 3G, loading fast.
   Overlay: "No flash. On purpose." 15s.
4. **A test run passing.** The terminal running the suite, hundreds of tests going green.
   Overlay: "The boring work that keeps your data safe." 15s. Dev content travels.
5. **"To work."** The intro card animating in (screen-record the card, slow zoom), isiZulu /
   isiXhosa pronunciation as text. 10s. Pin this Reel to the profile.

Rules: captioned in-app (most watch muted), our palette for overlays, one idea per Reel, no
trend audio, no real PII on screen.
