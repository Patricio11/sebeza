## 2 · Create the page, step by step

- [ ] 1. Log in to your personal Facebook account. A page is always owned through a
      personal profile; nobody sees which profile owns it.
- [ ] 2. **Before anything else: turn on two-factor authentication on your personal
      account** (Settings → Accounts Centre → Password and security). If the personal
      account is hijacked, the page goes with it. This is the Facebook version of our
      security posture.
- [ ] 3. Menu → Pages → **Create new page**.
- [ ] 4. Name: `Sebenza SA`. Category: type and pick `Software company`. Bio: paste
      from the table above. Create.
- [ ] 5. Contact: add `https://sebenzasa.com` and `info@sebenzasa.com`. Location:
      South Africa. Skip phone and hours.
- [ ] 6. Upload `assets/profile-picture.png` (profile) and `assets/cover-photo.png`
      (cover). Both are pre-sized; no cropping needed, accept as-is.
- [ ] 7. Claim the username: Page settings → search "username" → set `@sebenzasa`.
      (Facebook sometimes wants a few days of page age or 2FA first; if refused, retry
      after step 2 settles and a few posts exist.)
- [ ] 8. Add the action button: `Learn more` → `https://sebenzasa.com/marketing`.
- [ ] 9. In Settings, add `Employment agency` as an extra category.
- [ ] 10. Page transparency will show the page is managed from South Africa: correct
      and good, leave it.

## 3 · The first week (order matters)

- [ ] 1. **Do not invite anyone yet.** An empty page looks abandoned; three posts make
      it look alive. Warm it first.
- [ ] 2. Publish **Post 1** (the introduction) with `assets/post-01-intro.png`.
      **Pin it** (three dots on the post → Pin to top of page).
- [ ] 3. Two days later, publish **Post 2** (who is behind this) with a workspace or
      code-on-screen shot. No faces: the page speaks as "we" for now. The post bank
      has the we-voice caption ready.
- [ ] 4. Two days after that, publish **Post 3** (the one-liner) with a clean product
      screenshot (ask Claude for a branded screenshot frame when needed; never show
      real personal data).
- [ ] 5. NOW invite: personal friends first (page → ⋯ → Invite friends), then share
      Post 1 to your own profile and to WhatsApp. Ask five people you trust to follow
      and share honestly.
- [ ] 6. From here, the playbook cadence: 3 posts a week (Mon mission/value, Wed
      behind-the-scenes, Fri vision), reply to every comment, ten minutes of
      engagement daily. The post bank is your runway; the ready-made graphics are
      mapped inside it.

## 4 · Visual identity on Facebook (the law)

Everything visual comes from the product's own design system ("Mzansi National" /
Civic Editorial). The kit in `assets/` is pre-rendered with all of this baked in; the
rules matter when making anything new.

**Palette**

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Paper (cream) | `#fbf8f0` |
| Ink (charcoal) | `#14110d` |
| Ink soft | `#5a5249` |
| Flag green (brand) | `#006b3c` |
| Deep green (dark surfaces) | `#003d1f` |
| Gold (accent) | `#c98214` (bright `#f5a623` on dark surfaces) |
| Red (alerts + stripe only) | `#de3831` |

**Type**: Fraunces for display (the serif with personality; accent words italic in
gold), Hanken Grotesk for everything else. Never another font.

**The flag stripe**: green : gold : red at **3 : 2 : 1**, in that order, full-bleed at
the bottom of **every post graphic**. It is the product's own stripe (PWA icon, 404
page). Never reorder it, never change the ratio, never use it as a side bar. Details
and the dark-surface variant: `assets/README.md`.

**The marks**: the chevron person-mark + Fraunces wordmark is the logo lockup (post
footers, cover). The Fraunces "S" + short stripe is the avatar mark (profile picture,
matches the installed app icon, survives the circle crop). Never stretch, recolour
outside the palette, or set the wordmark in another font.

**House copy rules apply to social**: no em-dashes anywhere (captions, graphics,
replies); "Sebenza · sebenzasa.com" style separators use "·"; never name the
incumbent registry or any competitor; never invent numbers, users, or testimonials;
location, never nationality.

## 5 · What only you can supply

The kit covers every designed graphic. Three things need the founder, not the design
system:

1. **Behind-the-scenes photos** (posts 2, 8, 11): workspace, code on screen, the
   late-night desk, the coffee. **No faces for now**: the founder stays off-camera
   until they choose otherwise. (When that day comes, a "meet the person who's been
   building this" post will be the strongest post the page ever makes. Keep it in
   the pocket.)
2. **Your voice in the captions**: the bank's drafts are solid, but the VOICE files
   exist so the best posts sound human. One weirdly specific true detail beats any
   polish. Recast any "I" in them to "we".
3. **Product screenshots** (posts 3, 12, 15, 16, 18): ask Claude to produce branded
   screenshot frames from the live product when each post comes up, so they are
   current and PII-free.

## 6 · Guardrails (unchanged, from the playbook)

No sign-up CTAs and no paid ads until the gates clear. No invented traction. No
"solves unemployment" claims: we improve matching and visibility. Never disparage
any competitor by name. No real user PII in any screenshot. Tell the truth about
being early; it is the most differentiating thing this page has.

*Presence now. Story now. Followers now. The doors open later, and this page will be
warm when they do.*
