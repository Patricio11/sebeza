### X-4 · Free, always
**Card:** `../facebook/assets/post-06-free-always.png`

### X-5 · The honesty principle (plain text)
Our unpopular product decision: the "unverified" badge.

If we didn't verify something, the platform says so. No dressed-up self-reported data, no fake green ticks.

Trust is the whole product. We'd rather look smaller than lie.

### X-6 · Freshness (plain text)
Every talent database rots. People find work and their "available" profile stays up for years.

Sebenza ranks freshness: statuses carry a confirmation date, stale profiles sink honestly.

When an employer sees "available," it means now. Not 2024.

### X-7 · The cheap phone rule
**Card:** `../facebook/assets/post-09-cheap-phone.png`
Design constraint we refuse to break: Sebenza must work on a R1500 Android on 3G.

No heavy JS, no airtime-eating animations. The reference device is the phone our users actually own.

Fast and fair beats flashy.

### X-8 · Four languages (plain text)
One profile. English, isiZulu, isiXhosa, Afrikaans.

Not a translation plugin: human-reviewed copy for the words that matter (consent, privacy, legal). Machine translation of a consent form is not consent.

### X-9 · The data story (plain text)
Every search on a talent platform quietly reveals which skills employers want but cannot find, and where.

Aggregated and anonymised, that's a live skills-gap map of South Africa. Policy today runs on surveys that were stale before publication.

We're building the live version.

## THREADS (~1/week max)

### Thread A · Why we're building Sebenza (7 posts)
1/ South Africa doesn't have a talent shortage. It has a trustworthy-signal shortage. A short thread on why we're building a national talent platform. 🧵
2/ A hundred CVs land in a WhatsApp group. Nobody can tell who is real, who is available, who is nearby. The talent is there; the signal is gone.
3/ Existing databases rot. "Available" people found work months ago. Badges say verified when nobody verified anything. Everyone knows; everyone pretends.
4/ So the register we're building treats honesty as the feature: unverified means unverified, every status carries a freshness date, and a hire only counts when it's confirmed.
5/ Job-seekers pay nothing. Ever. They build one profile, in four languages, on any phone, and get found by skill and place.
6/ And because hiring happens through the platform, the aggregate picture is real: which skills are short, where, right now. K-anonymised, never an identifiable person.
7/ We're early and we'd rather be honest about that than pretend otherwise. Follow along as we build. 🇿🇦 sebenzasa.com

### Thread B · Honest data for an honest job market (5 posts)
1/ Everyone claims their hiring platform "has data." Here's what honest labour-market data actually requires. 🧵
2/ Confirmation, not collection: a hire has to be confirmed by the employer through the platform. Self-reported placements inflate; confirmed ones count once and count true.
3/ Freshness, not volume: a million stale profiles are worth less than ten thousand fresh ones. Every status on Sebenza carries a date, and ranking respects it.
4/ Privacy floors, not privacy theatre: analytics only ever leave the building k-anonymised and aggregate. No individual is ever visible in a statistic.
5/ Get those three right and the by-product is a live map of South Africa's skills gaps. That's the bigger reason Sebenza exists. sebenzasa.com
