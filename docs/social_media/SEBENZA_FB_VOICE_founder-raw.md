## HOW TO USE THIS FILE
Each item gives you a **hook angle** and a **prompt** with blanks only you can fill. Write it rough, fast,
in the notes app on your phone, like you're texting a friend who asked "so what are you actually building?"
Then post it. Don't polish it into a press release. Rough-but-yours beats smooth-but-generic every time.

## 🔥 FOUNDER-RAW PROMPTS (fill in your real life)

### A1 · The origin moment
**Angle:** the exact moment the idea became undeniable.
**Prompt to finish:** *"I can tell you the exact moment I decided to build Sebenza. I was ______ [where? doing what? who were you talking to?]. They told me ______ [what did they say about looking for work?]. And I thought: ______."*
→ The specific place and the specific person is what makes this real. Name them if you can.

### A2 · What I gave up
**Angle:** the honest cost of building alone.
**Prompt:** *"Things I've said no to while building this: ______ [weekends? a side gig? sleep? something specific you actually skipped]. People think building a startup is glamorous. Last night I was ______ at ______ [real time] trying to fix ______."*
→ The real time on the clock. The real thing that broke. Specifics.

### A3 · The night it worked
**Angle:** the small victory nobody saw.
**Prompt:** *"Nobody was awake to see it, but last ______ [when], the ______ [which feature] finally worked. I actually ______ [what did you do? stand up? say something out loud? text someone?]. Small thing. Felt huge."*

### A4 · Why me, an outsider to this problem
**Angle:** you're a developer, not an "employment expert." Own it.
**Prompt:** *"I'm not an unemployment expert. I'm a developer. So why am I building this? Because ______ [your real reason: who you kept meeting, what you kept seeing]. Sometimes the person who fixes a thing isn't the expert. It's just the one who couldn't walk past it."*

### A5 · The thing I refuse to do
**Angle:** a line you won't cross, stated plainly.
**Prompt:** *"There's one thing I will never do with Sebenza, no matter what: ______ [charge job-seekers? fake numbers? sell people's data?]. I'd rather ______ [the real trade-off] than ______. If that costs me, fine."*
→ Conviction gets comments. People respect a founder with a line.

### A6 · Ask them something you actually don't know
**Angle:** genuine question = genuine comments.
**Prompt:** *"Real question, because I'm building this for you and I'd rather ask than guess: ______ [something you genuinely need to know, e.g. 'when you look for work, what's the single most frustrating part?']. I read every reply. It changes what I build next."*
→ This is your highest-comment format. Use it often. Actually reply to everyone.

### A7 · Show the ugly early version
**Angle:** vulnerability + proof it's real.
**Prompt:** *"This is what Sebenza looked like ______ [when? 3 months ago?]. Rough, right? 😅 [screenshot]. Here's where it is now [screenshot]. Building in public means showing the ugly middle, not just the shiny end."*
→ Real screenshots. The contrast IS the post.

### A8 · The doubt
**Angle:** honest struggle (people root for this).
**Prompt:** *"Some days I wonder if one person building alone can actually make a dent in something as huge as ______. Then I remember ______ [what keeps you going: a specific person, a specific reason]. Back to it."*

### A9 · A feature nobody asked for but you're proud of
**Angle:** the unique differentiator, told personally.
**Prompt:** *"Most job platforms just help you find a job. I built something into Sebenza that I haven't seen anywhere: it looks at what employers near you actually want, and tells you ______ [what to learn to get there]. Because finding a job you're not ready for isn't help. Getting ready is."*

### A10 · The name
**Angle:** why "Sebenza," personally.
**Prompt:** *"People ask why I called it Sebenza. ______ [what it means to you, not just the dictionary. Why THAT word, why not an English name]."*

## ✅ MAKE-IT-SOUND-LIKE-YOU CHECKLIST (run every post through this)
- [ ] **One weirdly specific true detail** (a time, a place, a name, a number). This alone kills the AI smell.
- [ ] **Uneven rhythm.** Vary sentence length hard. One long rambling one. Then a short one. Like that.
- [ ] **Cut symmetrical emoji.** Max one or two, where you'd actually use them. Not one per line.
- [ ] **Kill the tricolon.** If you wrote three balanced clauses ("no X, no Y, just Z"), rewrite one.
- [ ] **Read it aloud.** Cut anything you wouldn't actually say to a friend. If it sounds like a brochure, it is one.
- [ ] **Leave it slightly rough.** A real typo or an unpolished line is more convincing than perfection.
- [ ] **NO em-dashes. Ever.** House rule, total ban: they read as AI. Use full stops. Or start a new line.
- [ ] **Would only YOU have written this?** If someone could swap in any other startup's name and it still works, it's too generic. Add the thing only you know.

*Rough, specific, and yours. That's the whole game.*
