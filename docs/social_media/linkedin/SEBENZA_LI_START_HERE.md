## 2 · Create it, step by step

A company page is created and administered from a personal LinkedIn profile; admins are
invisible to visitors, and **no posts ever go out as the person**: the page speaks as the page.

- [ ] 1. From your personal LinkedIn: For Business (grid icon) → **Create a Company Page**.
- [ ] 2. Fill the identity table above; claim the `sebenzasa` public URL.
- [ ] 3. Upload logo + banner. Verify the page (LinkedIn prompts for a workplace email:
      use `info@sebenzasa.com`).
- [ ] 4. Add the **Lead** call-to-action button: `Learn more` → `https://sebenzasa.com/marketing`.
- [ ] 5. First post: LI-1 from the bank. Then the cadence below.
- [ ] 6. Personal-profile hygiene (optional but powerful later): when the gates clear and
      you're ready to appear, your own profile "Building Sebenza" becomes the second channel.
      Until then the page stands alone.

## 3 · What LinkedIn is FOR in our system

This is the **B2B and institutional channel**: the one place where the audience is the
employer, the recruiter, the SETA official, the journalist, the potential partner. The seeker
story still leads (it's the heart), but posts here carry more precision: the verification
model, the data story, the POPIA posture.

Cadence: **1-2 posts per week**, quality over volume. LinkedIn's feed half-life is days, not
hours; fewer, denser posts win.

Three lanes:
1. **The mission, told to institutions** (why trustworthy signal matters to the economy).
2. **Product with proof**: a feature, why it exists, what rule it enforces (screenshots).
3. **Build updates**: monthly "what shipped" digests; engineering honesty reads as competence
   here.

## 4 · LinkedIn-specific craft

- **First two lines carry the post** (the fold sits ~2-3 lines in). Open with the claim, not
  the wind-up.
- **Longer is fine here** (150-300 words), but every paragraph must earn its place.
- **Cards attach beautifully**: the 1080x1350 cards render large and sharp in-feed. Use one on
  most posts.
- **3-5 hashtags at the very end**: `#SouthAfrica #Hiring #FutureOfWork #POPIA #SkillsDevelopment`
  rotate; `#Sebenza` always.
- **No engagement-bait** ("Agree?", "Thoughts?" as the whole ask) and no broetry (one-line
  paragraphs stacked for fake drama). Civic Editorial voice: plain, precise, calm.
- Links in posts are fine on LinkedIn (unlike IG); still use them sparingly; the CTA button
  carries the permanent link.
