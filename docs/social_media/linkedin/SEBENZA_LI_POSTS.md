### LI-1 · The introduction (post first)
**Card:** `../facebook/assets/post-01-intro.png`

Sebenza means "to work" in isiZulu and isiXhosa.

We are building South Africa's national talent platform. The idea is simple to say and hard to
build: job-seekers create one free profile and get found for their skills and their location;
employers search a live register instead of drowning in unread CVs; and confirmed hiring rolls
up into honest, anonymised labour-market intelligence.

South Africa does not have a talent shortage. It has a trustworthy-signal shortage: databases
where half the "available" people found work months ago, badges that say verified when nobody
verified anything, placement numbers nobody can audit.

We are building the trustworthy version, and we are building it in the open. Follow the page
for the journey.

### LI-2 · The verification model
**Card:** `../facebook/assets/post-10-trust.png`

An unpopular product decision we stand behind: the default badge on Sebenza is "unverified."

Most platforms dress up self-reported data. We went the other way: a badge only says verified
when something was actually verified, availability statuses carry a confirmation date and stale
ones sink in ranking, and a hire only counts when the employer confirms it through the
platform.

For employers this means one thing: when you see "available," it means now. When you see
"verified," someone checked. The register would rather look smaller than lie.

Trust is not a marketing claim. It is enforced in code, on every read.

#Sebenza #Hiring #Trust

### LI-3 · Free for seekers is a design decision, not a promotion

Sebenza is free for job-seekers. Permanently. That is not a launch discount; it is the
architecture of the register.

A national talent register only works if every South African can afford to be on it. The
moment seekers pay to be listed or ranked, the register stops representing the labour market
and starts representing who could pay. The data value collapses along with the fairness.

So the model is simple: seekers never pay; employer and institutional partnerships carry the
platform.

#Sebenza #SouthAfrica #Inclusion

### LI-4 · The POPIA posture

We built Sebenza POPIA-first from the first line of code, not as a compliance retrofit.

What that means concretely: consent is granular and per-purpose, never a blanket tick-box.
Public and search payloads are structurally redacted (no ID numbers, no documents, no raw
contact details). Contact is revealed only to verified employers, with consent, and every
reveal is written to a permanent audit log the seeker can see. Sensitive fields are encrypted
at the field level. Export and erasure are built in.

Handling South Africans' employment data is a responsibility before it is a product. If your
organisation cares about how this is done, we are happy to talk: info@sebenzasa.com.

#POPIA #Privacy #Sebenza

### LI-5 · The data story (for institutions)

Every search on a talent platform quietly answers a question policy-makers struggle with:
which skills do employers want but cannot find, and where?

Because hiring on Sebenza is confirmed in-platform, the aggregate picture is real rather than
survey-lagged: live demand by skill and province, supply against it, placement outcomes. All
of it k-anonymised and aggregate; never an identifiable person.

Surveys tell us where the labour market was. Registers, honestly run, can show where it is.

#SkillsDevelopment #SouthAfrica #FutureOfWork

### LI-6 · Built for the real South Africa
**Card:** `../facebook/assets/post-09-cheap-phone.png`

Our reference device is a R1500 Android on 3G.

Not because it is fashionable to say so, but because that is the phone the South African
labour market actually holds. So: a strict JavaScript budget on key routes, no
airtime-eating animations, four launch languages (English, isiZulu, isiXhosa, Afrikaans), and
an installable app that works on any phone.

Accessibility is not a feature tier. It is the floor.

#Sebenza #SAtech #Inclusion

### LI-7 · How employers will use it (soft)

The employer side of Sebenza, in one paragraph: search by skill, place, and availability;
see honest verification states and freshness; run vacancies that reverse-match candidates;
invite people to named roles or share a public apply link; and log the hire when it happens,
so your numbers and ours stay true.

No agency fees, no CV mountains, no guessing whether a profile is stale.

We are early-stage and deliberate about it. If you hire in South Africa and want to see the
employer side as it grows: info@sebenzasa.com.

### LI-8 · The languages decision

Machine translation of a consent form is not consent.

Sebenza launches in English, isiZulu, isiXhosa, and Afrikaans. The interface fallbacks are
engineered, but the words that matter most (consent, privacy, legal) are human-reviewed. A
person should understand exactly what they are agreeing to in the language they think in.

Small decision, big principle: dignity lives in the details.

#Sebenza #POPIA #SouthAfrica
