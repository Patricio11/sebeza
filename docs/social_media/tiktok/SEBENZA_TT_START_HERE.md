## 3 · What TikTok is FOR in our system

TikTok is the **discovery engine**: the only platform where an account with 12 followers can
reach 100 000 people if the video holds attention. The audience is the youngest of all five
platforms, which is exactly the youth-unemployment audience. Facebook warms a community;
TikTok finds one.

Three content lanes, all from the bank:
1. **Product proof** (V1, V2, V8, V9, V10): the platform doing something real on screen.
2. **Text-driven mission shorts** (V6, V12): pure story, big type, no product needed.
3. **VALUE** (V7 scams, and future CV/interview tips): the most shareable lane; TikTok
   rewards saves and shares above all.
Plus **photo posts** between videos (see §5).

## 4 · TikTok-specific craft

- **Hook on frame one.** The bank's scripts all start with the hook text already on screen.
- **Captions short**, 3-5 hashtags: `#Sebenza` + topical + one broad SA tag (`#Mzansi`,
  `#SouthAfrica`, `#SAJobs`). TikTok SEO reads the caption AND on-screen text: say the
  keywords out loud in overlays ("jobs", "skills", "South Africa").
- **Sound:** calm instrumental beds, trending-audio only if it is instrumental and neutral.
  No meme audio, no voiceover AI voices. Muted-viewer captions always.
- **Covers:** set each video's cover to its card from `assets/` so the profile grid looks
  designed (TikTok crops grid tiles to 3:4: the cover cards keep their title in the middle
  band for that reason).
- **Reply to comments with comments**, and to good questions with a follow-up video (TikTok's
  video-reply feature): that is the native community loop.

## 6 · What NOT to do here

- ❌ Dances, memes, trend-chasing, voice-cloned narration. The brand is institutional trust;
  one embarrassing video outlives fifty good ones.
- ❌ Reposting TikToks WITH the TikTok watermark to YouTube/IG (both platforms down-rank
  watermarked video). Export clean from the editor; the bank's videos are made platform-neutral.
- ❌ Engagement bait ("comment 'JOB' and we'll DM you"): that pattern is the scam aesthetic we
  warn people about in V7. Never.
