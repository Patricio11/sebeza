# SEBENZA FB · VOICE B: BOLD & COMMUNITY-PROVOCATIVE
**Hooks built to make people STOP, comment, argue, tag, and share. Scaffolding you finish, not paste-ready.**
*This voice provokes response. Every post has a built-in engagement mechanic: a question, a poll, a hot take, a "share yours."*

> **Read the de-AI checklist at the bottom first.** Provocative + generic = still ignored. Provocative +
> specific + a real opinion people can argue with = comments. The opinion has to be genuinely YOURS.

---

## HOW TO USE THIS FILE
Each hook is engineered to earn a response. Pick the mechanic, then put YOUR real opinion or question into
it. The sharper and more specific your actual view, the more people react. Don't hedge. A safe post gets
scrolled past; a post with a real spine gets 100 comments (some disagreeing, and that's the point:
disagreement is engagement). Always reply to the comments. That's half the algorithm and all the community.

## ✅ MAKE-IT-SOUND-LIKE-YOU CHECKLIST (run every post through this)
- [ ] **The opinion is genuinely yours.** Not a safe take. If you don't actually believe it, don't post it; people smell fake conviction.
- [ ] **One specific detail or number** somewhere (a real stat, a real example). Specifics beat slogans.
- [ ] **Ask ONE clear thing.** One question, one vote, one "tag someone." Not three; people do the first.
- [ ] **Uneven rhythm, short punches.** Provocative posts are punchy. Cut the smooth flowing sentences.
- [ ] **Max one-two emoji**, where they land. Not decorating every line.
- [ ] **NO em-dashes. Ever.** House rule, total ban: they read as AI. Full stops, commas, or a new line.
- [ ] **Read it aloud.** If it sounds like a slogan-generator, put a rougher, realer line in.
- [ ] **Reply to comments**, especially disagreement. A debate in your comments is the algorithm's dream.
- [ ] **Would only Sebenza post this?** If any employment brand could, sharpen it with your unique angle.

*Provoke, don't lecture. Ask, don't announce. Then show up in the comments. That's how a page comes alive.*
