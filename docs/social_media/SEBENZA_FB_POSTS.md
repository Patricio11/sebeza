- **Order:** start with 1 → 2 → 3 (intro sequence), then mix pillars freely. Never two same-pillar in a row.
- **Every post needs a visual.** Quote-cards (Canva + your green/gold palette) are the fastest.
- **Adapt the voice to yours** — these are drafts, not scripture. Your real voice > polished copy.
- **Reply to every comment** early on. Conversation grows pages faster than posting.
- **Swap in real screenshots/photos** wherever noted — proof it's real is your best content.
- **Hold all "sign up" CTAs** until the launch gates clear, then we write the "we're live" kit.
- **Recycle:** after ~10 weeks, the best performers can be re-posted with fresh visuals.
