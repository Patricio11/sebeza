# SEBENZA · MASTER POSTING CALENDAR
**The one file that says what to post, where, and when. Everything references ready-made
captions and assets; nothing here needs creating on the day.**

How to use it: work down the checklist, tick as you post. "Day 1, Day 3..." are counted from
whenever you start a week; the rhythm matters, not the exact dates. If you miss a day, shift
everything forward; never double-post to catch up.

**The weekly rhythm (after launch week):**

| Day | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Feed post (mission/value) | Facebook + Instagram (same card, IG caption trimmed) |
| Tuesday | One story | Instagram (reshare to FB story with one tap) |
| Wednesday | Feed post (behind-the-scenes) AND the week's short video | FB + IG feed · video to TikTok + YouTube Shorts + IG Reels |
| Thursday | One story + one X post | Instagram · X |
| Friday | Feed post (vision/product) + LinkedIn post | FB + IG · LinkedIn |
| Sat/Sun | Nothing scheduled. Ten minutes of replies only. | everywhere |

Captions: Facebook versions in `facebook/SEBENZA_FB_POSTS.md`, Instagram trims in
`instagram/SEBENZA_IG_POSTS.md`, X in `x/SEBENZA_X_POSTS.md`, LinkedIn in
`linkedin/SEBENZA_LI_POSTS.md`, video captions + covers in `tiktok/SEBENZA_VIDEO_BANK.md`.
Post mornings 6-8am or evenings 7-9pm. Reply to every comment, every day, ten minutes.
